BUSINESS CASE
============
Company: OmniRetail Analytics (synthetic)
Period: Jan-2024 to Aug-2026
Scope: Customer, sales, product, marketing and service analytics

Executive questions
-------------------
1. Which regions and categories drive revenue?
2. Which categories drive profit rather than just sales?
3. What percentage of customers are repeat customers?
4. Which customers are at risk based on revenue decline?
5. Which discount levels damage margin?
6. Which campaign channels create efficient conversions?
7. Which support issues create the biggest service burden?
8. What operational patterns should management act on?

Expected analytical outputs
---------------------------
Revenue trend, YoY growth, AOV, customer retention, RFM segmentation,
product profitability, discount/margin analysis, return rate, delivery performance,
support resolution/CSAT, campaign funnel and cost per conversion.

Definition of success
---------------------
A recruiter should be able to see:
- strong SQL
- practical Python/Pandas
- Power BI + DAX
- ETL/data quality
- data modeling
- business analysis
- ability to communicate recommendations
