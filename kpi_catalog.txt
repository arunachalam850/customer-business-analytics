KPI CATALOG
===========

Revenue = Sum of order_total for Completed and Returned orders
Orders = Distinct order_id for Completed and Returned
AOV = Revenue / Orders
Gross Profit = Net line sales - product cost
Gross Margin % = Gross Profit / Revenue
Return Rate % = Returned orders / Orders
Repeat Customer % = Customers with >1 valid order / valid customers
YoY Growth % = (Current period revenue - prior year revenue) / prior year revenue
Avg Delivery Days = Average delivery_days for valid orders
Avg Resolution Hours = Average support resolution_hours
Avg CSAT = Average support CSAT
CTR % = Clicks / Delivered
Conversion Rate % = Conversions / Clicks
Cost per Conversion = Spend / Conversions

Document every KPI in Power BI with a clear definition and filters.
