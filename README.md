# Customer & Business Analytics Platform

An end-to-end business analytics project demonstrating data preparation, SQL analysis, data modeling, KPI development, and Power BI reporting.

The project simulates a real-world business analytics environment where data from customers, products, orders, payments, returns, marketing activities, and customer support is transformed into actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed to demonstrate an end-to-end Data Analyst workflow:

**Raw Data → Python ETL → SQL Database → Data Modeling → Business Analysis → Power BI Dashboard**

The project focuses on answering common business questions related to:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Customer behavior
- Customer segmentation
- Product performance
- Profitability
- Customer retention
- Returns
- Marketing performance
- Customer support
- Business KPIs

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

1. Analyze overall business and sales performance.
2. Understand customer purchasing behavior.
3. Identify valuable customer segments.
4. Analyze product and category performance.
5. Measure profitability and business KPIs.
6. Analyze customer retention and potential churn.
7. Evaluate returns and operational performance.
8. Analyze marketing and customer-support activities.
9. Build an interactive Power BI reporting solution.
