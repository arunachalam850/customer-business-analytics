POWER BI REPORT DESIGN
======================

Page 1: Executive Overview
--------------------------
KPI cards: Revenue | Gross Profit | Margin % | Orders | AOV | Customers | Return Rate
Visuals: Monthly Revenue & YoY, Revenue by Region, Revenue by Category, Sales Channel Mix
Slicers: Date, Region, Segment, Category, Channel

Page 2: Customer Analytics
--------------------------
KPI: Customers | Repeat Customer % | Avg Customer Revenue
Visuals: New vs Returning, Customer Segment Mix, RFM matrix, Top Customers, Revenue by Segment
Drill-through: Customer Detail

Page 3: Product & Profitability
-------------------------------
KPI: Revenue | Gross Profit | Margin %
Visuals: Category Revenue vs Margin, Top/Bottom Products, Discount Band vs Margin, Pareto 80/20
Drill-through: Product Detail

Page 4: Operations & Service
----------------------------
KPI: Return Rate | Avg Delivery Days | Avg Resolution Hours | Avg CSAT
Visuals: Returns by Reason, Delivery Trend, Support Issue Mix, Resolution Time by Priority

Page 5: Marketing Performance
----------------------------
KPI: Spend | Conversions | Cost per Conversion | CTR
Visuals: Campaign Funnel, Campaign ROI/Cost per Conversion, Channel Comparison, Conversion Trend

Business insight callouts:
- Highlight high-revenue / low-margin categories.
- Highlight customers at risk of revenue decline.
- Highlight campaigns with high spend and weak conversion.
- Highlight service issues with high volume + poor CSAT.
